# llm.txt — LastWinner 2026 vault-drain investigation

You are reading a research repo. This file orients an LLM (or a human in a hurry) so you can
continue the investigation without re-deriving what is already known. Ground truth is on-chain;

====================================================================
FACT / INTERPRETATION / ALTERNATIVES  (the 30-second version)
====================================================================
FACT (on-chain):
 - Since May 2026 one entity has swept ~44,000 wallets tied to the 2018 game LastWinner:
   loose EOA ETH (~3,000 ETH) + in-contract game vaults via withdraw() (~1,545 ETH;
   contract 3,970.71 -> 2,425.63 ETH).
 - ~80-85% of the swept wallets provably touched the LastWinner contract; ~2/3 were funded
   by Huobi/HTX + BW.com withdrawals; ~44% used a Nov-2018 ETH pooling contract ("coToken").
 - ~4,550 ETH moved; mostly PARKED as of early Sep 2026 (2,907 ETH unmoved in 0xe4a219fbed).
 - A SEPARATE operator (0xA707, publicly reported Apr-30) swept ~11-16 of the same wallets
   days earlier; its other 561 victims are unrelated.
 - ~1,000 still-unclaimed vaults belong to 2018 contracts that self-destructed (no code,
   no key) - permanently stuck.
WE THINK:
 - The ~44k wallets are one 2018 LastWinner farming operation (or mostly one).
 - The 2026 sweep is slightly more likely a consolidation by the rightful keyholder than a
   theft (methodical, works empty wallets, parks not dumps). ~50/50.
 - Keys probably exposed by weak 2018 key generation, not a targeted breach.
COULD BE INSTEAD:
 - A mix: one farm + independent farmers + retail.
 - A theft (phishing label; part of the April-2026 weak-key wave; a real user claimed a
   wallet). Tips this way if the parked ETH cashes out or the key flaw is reproducible.
 - 2-3 parties / a sweeper service, not one person.
 - "Funded by Huobi" could just mean ordinary Chinese gamblers, not a coordinated farm.

CONFIDENCE / WHAT IS DISPUTED
====================================================================
- FACT: one entity controls the whole ~44k fleet and is draining it (uniform gas-drip from
  shared funders, same-minute gas->withdraw()->sweep, shared rotating collectors).
- PLAUSIBLE but NOT PROVEN: the fleet is one 2018 operation. Support: ~44% used the coToken
  pool; shared Aug+Nov 2018 creation windows; the uniform 2026 drain. Against: 'shared funders'
  turned out to be exchange hot wallets (weak); ~15% never touched LastWinner; behaviour is
  heterogeneous. Could be one farm, or a farm + independent farmers + retail.
- NO identified private bankroll entity. The wallets thought to be one were HTX/BW hot wallets.
- OPEN, ~50/50: is the 2026 drainer the rightful keystore holder (consolidation) or someone
  who obtained the keys (theft)? For consolidation: an ORGANISED ~44k-key roster worked
  methodically, unhurried park-don't-cash-out behaviour (2,907 ETH unmoved 40+ days), the
  ~44% coToken-pool subset. For theft: public "hack" framing, Etherscan phishing label, at
  least one affected user claiming a wallet, part of a broader April-2026 weak-key wave.
  The 2026-03-14 mass sweep of the six funding wallets is NOT a data point here - it was HTX
  hot-wallet housekeeping.
- Numbers: withdraw() extraction 1,545 ETH is RPC-reconciled exact (3,970.71 -> 2,425.63).
  Track-A "2,907 ETH" is one on-chain transfer. "44,014 drained" is a union of confirmed
  withdraw() callers + confirmed track-A senders (see scripts).

OPEN QUESTIONS (ranked) + HOW TO ATTACK EACH
====================================================================
1. THE KEY-COMPROMISE VECTOR. How were ~44k private keys obtained? Candidates: (a) weak RNG in
   the 2018 wallet generator / LastWinner app (cf. SECBIT "Trust Wallet's Fomo3D Summer",
   srand(time(NULL))); (b) one master HD seed / keystore file; (c) a leaked custodial backend.
   -> Get the 2018 LastWinner Android/iOS app binary or its front-end JS (site lastwinner.me is
      dead; try web.archive.org CDX for lastwinner.me/download.html and assets/lw.js c.2018).
   -> If a cracked-key structure is ever published (e.g. "top N bits zero", "keccak(seed|counter)"),
      test it against data/drained_eoas.csv.
   -> Distinguishes "finite exposure" (a file) from "open-ended" (a reproducible RNG bug).

2. CONSOLIDATION vs THEFT. Would flip toward theft if the vector is a reproducible weak RNG
   (anyone can regenerate keys). Would flip toward consolidation if it's a single keystore only
   the operators held. Also: watch 0xe4a219fbed (2,907 ETH) — a fast bridge/exchange move =
   more theft-like; continued parking / redeployment = more owner-like.

3. WHO OPERATED THE 2018 FLEET? No private bankroll entity identified (the wallets thought to
   be one are HTX/BW hot wallets). Is it one farm, or farmers + retail? Attack: map the coToken
   pool's 552 depositors (data would need pulling) and see how much of the drained set they
   cover; cluster the fleet by 2nd-hop funder (the wallet that funded the Huobi-withdrawal
   recipient is more informative); check the Ant Swarm promoter wallets if 2018 Chinese sources
   name any. The deployer 0xeae69cad is a throwaway with no links.

4. SIZE THE PERMANENTLY-STUCK ETH. Of the 2,425 ETH still in the contract, how much is in
   self-destructed 2018 contracts (unrecoverable) vs live EOAs (recoverable iff a key
   exists)? Method: for each address in data/unclaimed.json, Etherscan getcontractcreation
   (was it ever a contract) + eth_getCode (alive now). Sampling so far: ~700 of the top-900
   unclaimed by value (~440 ETH) are self-destructed 2018 contracts. This scan was started and
   not finished (rate limits) — finish it.

5. CHARACTERISE THE NON-LASTWINNER ~15%. ~6,500 drained wallets never called the contract.
   Are they proxy-mediated players (their contract played), pure reserve wallets, or used for
   other 2018 ventures? Check their 2018 outbound for other game contracts / DEXes.

6. DOWNSTREAM OF THE PARKED ETH. 0xe4a219fbed (2,907), 0x8d7c20e3 (102), 0x5723168b (~62),
   0x220ecbb6 (20). None cashed out as of early Sep 2026. Monitor. Also identify
   0xa03400e098 = a live HTX hot wallet (nonce ~980k); the 2026-03-14 sweep to it was HTX housekeeping.

7. RELATION TO THE WIDER APRIL-2026 DORMANT-WALLET WAVE. 0xA707 is one of several drainers in
   that wave (WazzCrypto/Specter). Are the others also generic weak-key sweepers, or is there a
   coordinated campaign? Cross-reference 0xA707's 561 non-LastWinner victims (data/a707_senders_full.json)
   against other flagged drainers.

====================================================================
GOTCHAS
====================================================================
- Alchemy/Infura free tier: eth_getLogs is capped (10-block range) and JSON-RPC batching may be
  refused. eth_call / eth_getBalance / eth_getCode single calls are fine. Use Etherscan for
  transaction history; it pages ~1000 rows/query and rate-limits at ~3-5 req/s.
- balances.json (the per-player vault snapshot) is NOT in this repo. It was an external input. 
  Regenerate via getPlayerInfoByAddress(address) per pID, or from event logs.
- FoMo3D pID<->address mechanics: the address bound to a player-ID is set on first interaction
  (determinePID: plyr_[pID].addr = msg.sender). Re-pointing an existing pID to a new address is
  PERMISSIONED — you must already control an address on that pID (PlayerBook addMeToGame /
  registerNameX*). No admin override, no permissionless claim. We found NO pID re-pointing in
  this operation; the drain calls withdraw() straight from the compromised 2018 EOAs.
- Some "nonce 0 at block X" reads are misleading: a self-destructed contract also has nonce 0
  and no later txs. Confirm dead-contract status with getcontractcreation + eth_getCode.
- Vanity look-alikes: 0xa707... and 0x5723... prefixed addresses send 0-value address-poisoning
  txs at victims. Ignore 0-value transfers when following the money.


====================================================================
ASSUMPTIONS / CONCLUSIONS THAT COULD BE WRONG — CONFRONT THESE
====================================================================
(Every item is a place a plausible-looking read could be wrong. E.g. the six wallets that fund
 most of the fleet look like a private "bankroll" but are HTX/BW exchange hot wallets. Verify
 everything against chain.)

A. "The ~44,014 drained addresses are ONE 2018 farming operation." PLAUSIBLE, not proven.
   Could be: one farm + independent small farmers + retail, all lumped together because they
   (a) played LastWinner and (b) got drained in 2026. The set is a UNION of "2026 withdraw()
   callers" + "track-A loose-ETH senders" - defensible as "the drained set", weaker as "one
   fleet". Only ~44% used the coToken pool; the other ~56% could be anyone.

B. "One entity controls all 44k keys." Strong (uniform gas-drip, same-minute
   gas->withdraw()->sweep, shared rotating collectors) but could be 2-3 cooperating parties,
   or a sweeper/"recovery" SERVICE acting for multiple key-owners. The 0xA707 case proves
   multiple unrelated parties can hold overlapping keys.

C. "~1,545 ETH via withdraw()." RPC-reconciled (3,970.71 -> 2,425.63) so solid - but assumes
   ~0 ETH flowed INTO the contract in the window (checked: 10 dust buyXid txs). A selfdestruct
   into the contract or a mis-attributed internal tx could shift it slightly.

D. "2,907 ETH parked, nothing cashed out." TRUE ONLY AS OF EARLY SEP 2026. It's a snapshot.
   Re-check 0xe4a219fbed / 0x8d7c20e3 / 0x5723168b / 0x220ecbb6 before relying on it - a
   later bridge/mixer/exchange move flips the consolidation-vs-theft read.

E. "Dead 2018 contracts are a large share of the stuck 2,425 ETH." SAMPLE-BASED (29/30 of
   the top-900 nonce-0 unclaimed = self-destructed). The full scan of data/unclaimed.json was
   NOT finished. The exact permanently-stuck amount is an estimate.

F. "80.7% of the drained set touched LastWinner." The caller scan converged only through
   ~mid-Sep 2018. The 19% "unknown" is really "not confirmed" - a full-history sample of that
   group found 60% DID call the contract (Sep-Nov 2018). "Never touched" is ~15%, itself an estimate.

G. "0xA707 overlap is incidental." Based on a 120-sample of its 561 non-LastWinner victims
   (spanning 2017-2026, not Huobi-2018-funded). If a larger sample showed 0xA707's victims
   ARE the same 2018 Huobi-funded cohort, the "shared keystore" reading revives.

H. "coToken is not the game operator." Based on ZERO direct txs between the coToken deployer
   (0xcbeb72c160) and the LastWinner deployer/contract. Absence of a direct link != no
   relationship. coToken's 552 depositors were NOT checked against any operator wallets.

I. "The game deployer 0xeae69cad tells us nothing." It's minimal (23 tx) but its ~5 payout
   counterparties (0xccd8c1134b, 0xf8a80292ac, 0x531a4e109807, ...) were not traced - one could
   be an operator wallet.

J. 2018 identity claims ("Ant Swarm pyramid", "~200k ETH bot funding") are from SECBIT / BlockBeats - NOT
   independently re-verified here.

K. "Consolidation vs theft ~50/50" is a JUDGEMENT, not a measurement. Weighting "public hack
   framing + phishing label" against "parks-don't-dump + organised roster" is subjective;
   a reasonable analyst could land 70/30 either direction.

L. Meta: this may be a known / benign operation (a licensed recovery service, or the operators
   openly reclaiming) that merely looks dramatic on-chain. No comms / attribution confirm it either way.
