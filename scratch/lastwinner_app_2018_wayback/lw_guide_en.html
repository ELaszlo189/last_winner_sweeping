  • What is a smart contract?
    There are two kinds of accounts in Ethereum, one is the normal account for human operation, the other is the wallet account, and the other is the smart account. In the normal account, there is only the current ETH money, while the smart account stores two. Kind of thing: state and code, each time a message is received, the code is executed, changing its state. These accounts are so-called smart contract carriers. Simply put, a smart contract is the code that is deployed to the Ethereum smart account and requires external messages to trigger the code execution. Once the code is deployed, it cannot be changed, and the code is open source and can be viewed freely on Etherscan.io.

3. Game Overview

  • This is a game in which the grand prize winner is the last person to purchase a seed before the countdown timer runs down to zero.
  • During a round, a timer is constantly counting down from 3:00 to zero. Every time a seed is purchased, the buyer becomes the new "holder of the private seeds" and additional time is added to the countdown. The price of the seeds will now start to get slightly more expensive with each purchase. When the timer reaches zero, the last person to have bought a seed wins the round!
  • At the end of the round, the ETH in the pot is divvied up: the winner receives 47% of the pot,with the rest split between all LW players as well as to next round.The specific way the ETH is divided between the LW player and to next round pot depends on which team the winner was representing.
  • Once the round ends, the game has a 15 minutes grace period with transactions sent to the game passing through to your Dividend and Seed purchasing is disabled. This lets players realize what happened, and get into position to buy in early on the next round.

4. Game Steps

  • Game Ready
    • The first time you start LW (Last Winner) you need to create a game account. LW uses the wallet as account system. Players can create or import an Ethereum wallet as a game account.
    • The way to create a new account will generate an Ethereum wallet for the player and register the game account in the contract.
    • The way to import the account will use the player's existing Ethereum wallet to register the game account in the contract. The way to import accounts can also be used to recover and reset PIN codes for LW accounts. Players can import wallets using mnemonics and private Seeds.
    • Whether you are creating or importing an account, you need to set a wallet PIN for payment verification. LW does not store user PIN code, can not provide recovery function, please keep in mind.
    • New account registration can must input the referral code (pure number), then click Finish to create LW game account.
    • After creating an account, enter the main game interface. If the current game has not started, wait patiently and pay attention to the official notice.
    • If the administrator has activated the game start program in the contract, the player can see that the game starts counting down. After the countdown is 0, the game officially begins.
    • Suggestion: Pre-transfer some ETH to the wallet address corresponding to your game account before the game starts, as a game reserve fund. If your EHT is on the exchange, you can withdraw it to the game account; if your ETH is on another wallet address, you can use the third-party wallet tool to send to the game account.
  • Purchase seed
    • In the "Purchase Seed" menu on the main interface of the game, enter or select the number of Seeds you need to purchase, select the team you want to join, then click "Send ETH" and complete the payment. Waiting for the transaction to confirm the successful transaction on the blockchain, you can get the corresponding number of Seeds in the game.
    • Players who have already received dividends can also choose to "Use Dividend", that is, purchase the Seed with the dividends already obtained. This operation requires a 0ETH transaction trigger to be sent to the contract. After waiting for the transaction to succeed, the player has received dividends and will be converted into seeds.
  • Dividend Withdraw
    • Players can withdraw the dividends they have received to the wallet, which requires a 0ETH transaction trigger to be sent to the contract. After waiting for the transaction to succeed, you can check the balance changes in your wallet.
    • There is no limit on the withdrawal, you can withdraw it at any time.
  • Every time you purchase Seed, withdraw , activate the invitation system, you will initiate a transaction request. This requires you to pay the miner fee to the Ethereum network miners. You can set the Gas limit and Gas Price freely. It is recommended to pay as much miner fee as possible to ensure the transaction speed, especially when Ethereum has a large trading volume (the miners preferentially package transactions that pay high miner fee).

5. The Teams

  • In Last Winner, there are three major teams that players can associate themselves with.
  • Every time you purchase an additional seed(s), you can select one of these three teams. The team you select represents both how you want your seed buy-in price split up as well as the pot distribution if you win the round once the timer runs out. In particular, each team is explained below:
    • Snake－I Want Dividend:Your seed buy-in price will have a larger distributed to LW players and somewhat less given to pot. After a round ends, a larger portion of the pot is distributed to LW players and somewhat less given to pot of next round.
    • Cook－I Want Final Award:Your seed buy-in price will have a larger given to pot and somewhat less distributed to LW players. After a round ends, a larger portion of the pot is given to pot of next round and somewhat less distributed to LW players .
    • Bull－All want:Half of the ETH you spend on purchasing seeds will be distributed to LW players and another half given to pot. After a round ends, half of the pot is distributed to LW players and another half given to pot of next round.
  • At the end of a round, after the countdown timer drops to zero, the winning team determines specifically how the ETH is distributed between the winner, all LW players from this round, and the remainder is left over for the next round. The winning team is the one selected during the purchase of the very last Seed in the round.

6. Pot Distribution

  • As explained above, at the end of each round, the ETH pot labeled as "Active Pot" is distributed between many parties. The exact pot distribution breakdown is always as follows:
    • 3% of the active pot goes to the community bank fund, which pays out great community members who make this game possible.
    • 47% of the active pot goes directly to the round winner's vault for them to be able to withdraw anytime!
    • The remaining 50% is distributed based on the winner's final team selection, with the following distribution possible:
Team Kept for next round pot LW Players
Snake 10% 40%
Cook 40% 10%
Bull 25% 25%
  • Let's take an example for clarity: suppose the "Active Pot" was at 100ETH when the final seed was purchased before the timer reaches zero and the winner chose Team Snake. The winner would receive 47% of the pot (47 ETH) directly into their vault. Since the winner chose Team Snake, 10% of the pot's ETH (10 ETH) is kept for the next round, 40% of the pot's ETH (40 ETH) goes to other LW players.
  • Kept for the next round means that the corresponding proportion of bonuses will be directly invested in the next round of the initial pot.

8. Random Airdrops

  • When purchasing seeds in the "Purchase" tab, you'll notice some text alerting you to the chance of an "airdrop". For example, the text might read: "Purchases of 0.1ETH or more have a 0.6% chance to win some of the 0.31 ETH airdrop pot, instantly!".
  • 10% of all incoming ETH goes into an airdrop side-pot. The global chance of receiving an airdrop starts at 0% and increases by 1% for every seed order (or purchase) over 0.1 ETH.Each time you purchase seeds, you have a % chance of receiving a portion of the airdrop side-pot ETH siphoned directly into your Dividend,and not extra seeds.
  • Once your purchase transaction completes on the blockchain, you'll be alerted if the airdrop multiplier was successful on your purchase.
  • If you win an airdrop, the amount of the airdrop-pot you get depends on how much ETH you risked to win the airdrop chance:
    • Purchase 0.1-1 ETH, and airdrop wins you 10% of the airdrop side-pot
    • Purchase 1-10 ETH, and airdrop wins you 30% of the airdrop side-pot
    • Purchase 10+ ETH, and airdrop wins you 60% of the airdrop side-pot
  • Be aware that you are competing with other players to receive these airdrops. Once any player receives the airdrop, the chance for everyone resets!
